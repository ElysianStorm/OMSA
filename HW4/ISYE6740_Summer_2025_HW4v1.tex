\]
where $\sigma (x) = 1/(1+e^{-x})$ is the sigmoid function, $z^i$ is a two-dimensional vector such that  $z_1^i = \sigma(\alpha^T x^i)$, and $z_2^i = \sigma(\beta^T x^i)$.
